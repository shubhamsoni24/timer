About Study Stopwatch Pro

This advanced stopwatch helps you track your study sessions using the Pomodoro technique with enhanced features.

Features:

  • Timer with visual progress indicator
  • Customizable study and break durations
  • Auto-start options for seamless transitions
  • Session history with detailed statistics
  • Notes system for capturing ideas
  • Task checklist with priority levels
  • Dark/light theme toggle
  • Export session data

Stay focused and productive with all-in-one study tracking!
